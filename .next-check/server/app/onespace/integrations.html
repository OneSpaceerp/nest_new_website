THE STARTING POINT

A connection is only useful when the handover is dependable.

An online order should reach the right team. A payment should reconcile to the right record. A rejected invoice should be visible to someone who can resolve it. We design integrations around these outcomes, including the situations where data is incomplete or a service is unavailable.

01

Define the source of truth

Before connecting systems, we agree which one owns customers, items, prices, stock, and transactions. Synchronisation timing, duplicate handling, permissions, and reconciliation are part of the scope.

  • Websites, commerce, payments, and specialist applications
  • Data mapping and record ownership
  • Retries, status reporting, and exception handling
02

Prepare for Egyptian e-invoicing

ETA integration is a separate workstream from standard ERP setup. We assess taxpayer onboarding, credentials, electronic-signature requirements, item and tax mappings, and the applicable document flow with your finance team.

  • Readiness review and integration scope
  • Document mapping, signing, and submission testing
  • Acceptance or rejection tracking and reconciliation
03

Keep e-receipts and e-invoices distinct

Point-of-sale receipts and business invoices have different integration flows. We confirm the required scope against the applicable ETA specifications and your organisation’s requirements. Software connection alone does not establish tax compliance.

  • Separate assessment of invoice and receipt workflows
  • Test scenarios for corrections and failed submissions
  • Documented operational ownership after launch
04

Build only the missing application

Where standard ERP workflows do not fit, a focused Frappe application or integration can cover the gap. We define acceptance tests, permissions, deployment, and ongoing maintenance before development begins.

  • Custom forms, portals, and approval flows
  • Reports tied to agreed business definitions
  • Version compatibility and upgrade planning

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions. Clear answers.

Is Egyptian e-invoicing built into every ERPNext setup?+

Do not assume it is. The connector, installation, onboarding, configuration, signing arrangement, testing, and ongoing maintenance must be identified in the project scope.

Does an integration guarantee tax compliance?+

No. The system must be configured for your organisation and applicable requirements, and documents need correct business data. Confirm tax treatment and obligations with your finance and tax advisers.

Can you connect an existing third-party tool?+

We review its supported interfaces, access permissions, rate limits, and data model. This determines the available approach, implementation effort, and maintenance requirements.
