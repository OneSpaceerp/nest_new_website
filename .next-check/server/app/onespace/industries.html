THE STARTING POINT

Your industry gives us a starting point. Your operation gives us the brief.

Two companies in the same sector can have very different approval rules, pricing models, data quality, and reporting needs. We use industry workflows to make discovery concrete, then validate the details with the people who will use the system.

FIND YOUR WORKFLOW

Built around the work you do.

Trading & distribution+

An order arrives across a growing catalogue. Connect price lists, purchasing, warehouses, deliveries, and collections so commercial commitments reflect stock reality.

  • Item and unit-of-measure consistency
  • Batch, serial, and warehouse visibility
  • Customer credit and outstanding balances

Manufacturing+

A delivery promise depends on material availability and the work already in progress. Connect demand, bills of materials, production activity, and cost records.

  • Material requirements and purchase planning
  • Work orders, job cards, and quality checks
  • Finished goods and production cost visibility
See how implementation works →
Retail & e-commerce+

Customers meet the same business in store and online. Define how catalogues, orders, fulfilment, returns, and payments move between the ERP and each sales channel.

  • Product and price ownership across channels
  • Stock and order synchronisation rules
  • Returns, settlement, and receipt requirements
See how implementation works →
Projects & professional services+

Work is progressing, but the financial picture is scattered. Bring estimates, tasks, time, expenses, and billing together around a shared project record.

  • Project budgets and cost centres
  • Task ownership and timesheets
  • Milestone or time-based billing workflows
See how implementation works →
Construction & contracting+

Each project carries its own purchasing, site costs, and payment arrangements. Start with the core financial and project records, then scope specialist contract workflows.

  • Project purchasing and material movements
  • Cost reporting by site or project
  • Separately scoped certificates, retention, and variations
See how implementation works →
Education & training+

Administration spans enrolment, schedules, fees, and staff. Assess the fit of education applications alongside the financial system before defining the rollout.

  • Student and programme data structure
  • Fee collection and financial reconciliation
  • Application fit and role-based access review
See how implementation works →
Healthcare operations+

Purchasing, stock, assets, and finance need dependable records. Clinical workflows require a separately assessed healthcare application and explicit data-access boundaries.

  • Consumables, batches, and expiry workflows
  • Department costs and equipment records
  • Clinical integrations and privacy requirements scoped separately
See how implementation works →
Hospitality, logistics & membership businesses+

Reservations, shipment events, or recurring memberships may live in specialist tools. Define what stays in those systems and what should connect to finance and operations.

  • Specialist-system integration assessment
  • Billing, purchasing, and operational cost tracking
  • Clear ownership of customer and service records

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions. Clear answers.

Are these ready-made industry products?+

These are workflow starting points for discovery, not a claim that every sector has a prebuilt OneSpace product. The proposal identifies standard features, additional applications, integrations, and custom work.

Can multiple branches or entities be included?+

We assess company structure, warehouses, permissions, reporting, and intercompany needs during discovery. The design and rollout plan then reflect the entities and locations in scope.
