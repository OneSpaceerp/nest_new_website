THE STARTING POINT

The value is in the connections.

An invoice is more useful when it connects to the customer order, the goods delivered, and the payment still due. ERPNext helps teams follow those relationships across business functions. The implementation makes sure your records, permissions, and working habits support that connected view.

CONNECTED CAPABILITIES

Across the working day.

Finance & accounting

Connect invoices, payments, receivables, payables, and financial reporting. Agree the chart of accounts, cost centres, opening balances, and approval responsibilities before importing data.

Sales & customer relationships

Follow an enquiry through quotation, order, delivery, and invoice. Keep customer details and commercial terms consistent across the handover to operations.

Purchasing

Turn material needs into supplier enquiries, purchase orders, receipts, and invoices. Define who can request, approve, and receive each type of purchase.

Stock & warehouses

Track receipts, transfers, deliveries, and stock valuation across locations. Use batch or serial records where needed, supported by disciplined item data and stock reconciliation.

Manufacturing

Bring bills of materials, work orders, material movements, job cards, and quality checks into the production flow. Confirm planning assumptions with the team responsible for the shop floor.

Projects & services

Organise tasks, timesheets, project costs, and billing around the work being delivered. Agree how progress and profitability will be reviewed.

Assets & maintenance

Maintain asset records, depreciation settings, and maintenance activities. Give finance and the people using equipment a consistent record to work from.

People & HR

Extend the scope with Frappe HR for employee records, leave, attendance, and payroll workflows. Local payroll rules and any attendance-device connections need their own validation.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions. Clear answers.

Does open source mean there are no project costs?+

No. Open source describes the software licensing model. Professional implementation, hosting, integrations, maintenance, and training remain separate cost items.

Are all Frappe applications included?+

No. The installed applications and their compatibility are specified in the scope. A shared ecosystem does not mean every application is installed or connected automatically.

Can you help compare ERPNext, Odoo, and Oracle?+

Yes. We compare the required workflows, licensing, integration effort, hosting options, and ongoing ownership. Products and editions have different capabilities, so the comparison starts with a requirements brief.
