BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is OneSpace?+

OneSpace is Nest Software Development’s ERP service brand. We help businesses select, implement, adapt, and support business systems. ERPNext is a core route; Odoo and Oracle requirements can also be discussed during platform selection.

Is OneSpace a separate ERP product?+

OneSpace is the service relationship around your ERP project. The underlying platform, included applications, configuration, custom work, hosting, and support are identified explicitly in your proposal.

Is ERPNext free?+

ERPNext is open-source software. Implementation, data preparation, custom development, hosting, and support still require investment. We separate those costs so you can compare the complete project.

Can we start with a few departments?+

Yes. A first phase can focus on the highest-priority workflows, provided the data and accounting dependencies are understood. Later phases can extend the same operating foundation.

Can you support Arabic and Egyptian requirements?+

We assess language, document layouts, accounting setup, and local integration needs during discovery. Egyptian e-invoicing is a separately scoped integration, with readiness and acceptance testing agreed before rollout.
